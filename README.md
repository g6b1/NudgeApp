# Nudge

**Your Gentle Everyday Assistant**  
*A little push toward a better day.*

Nudge is a calm, privacy-first personal assistant that helps you organize everyday life without the pressure of traditional productivity apps. It combines daily planning, routines, checklists, a calendar, a private journal, and thoughtful self-care tools into one cohesive experience — designed especially for people who deal with low energy, overwhelm, anxiety, executive dysfunction, or simply want a kinder way to get through the day.

Nudge never shames you for inactivity. It celebrates small positive actions, offers “bare minimum” and “just one thing” modes for hard days, and keeps everything local by default. Optional AI features can help generate routines or break tasks down, but the rest of the app works fully offline and without an account.

---

## Core Philosophy

Productivity should never come at the expense of well-being.

Nudge is useful whether you want to fully organize your life or simply get out of bed, brush your teeth, and do one small thing. Progress is measured in **Positive Actions** — individual completed steps, not perfect streaks or all-or-nothing goals.

> “What did you do for yourself?”  
> Not “How productive were you?”

## Features

### Today (Home)
- Time-aware greeting and a short calming message
- Compact daily overview with positive actions and completion insight
- Rotating motivational tips focused on gentleness and starting small
- Quick access to Start My Day, 10-Second Start, Brain Dump, Add Task, Calendar, and Nudge AI
- Expandable sections for today’s routines, checklists, scheduled items, and backpacks

### Routines
- Create, edit, reorder, duplicate, and archive routines
- Multiple versions per routine (Full, Normal, Quick, Low Energy, Bare Minimum)
- Guided mode that walks you through one activity at a time
- Scheduling and recurrence support
- “Start My Day” flow and Checked Off history with undo

### Checklists & Backpacks
- Flexible checklists with templates, colors, emojis, and completion history
- Reusable packing lists / backpacks (work, school, gym, travel, everyday essentials)
- Quantities, required items, missing-item indicators, and links to related routines

### Calendar
- Fully functional Month, Week, and Day views
- Schedule tasks, routines, and events with colors, descriptions, and reminders
- Advanced recurrence options and the ability to edit single occurrences

### Journal
- Private daily journal with mood tracking and optional prompts
- Searchable history linked to calendar dates
- Local-only storage; never sent to AI without explicit consent

### Positive Actions & Progress
- Counts real completed steps (not entire routines)
- Today / week / month summaries and category breakdowns
- No streaks, no guilt, no penalties for quiet days
- Supportive messaging on low-activity weeks

### Depression-Friendly Tools
- Energy modes (High → Very Low)
- Bare Minimum Day
- Just One Thing
- Nudge Me
- Make This Easier (task breakdown)
- Get Me Out of Bed
- Simplified hygiene and gentle eating support
- Warm “welcome back” messaging after time away

### Nudge AI (Optional)
- Natural-language generation of routines, checklists, and backpacks
- Task breakdown and Brain Dump analysis
- Daily planning suggestions
- Conversational help with organization and prioritization
- Requires explicit consent; fully optional; clear disclosure when data is sent externally

### Brain Dump, Timer & Focus
- Fast capture of thoughts with conversion to tasks/reminders/checklists
- Configurable 10-Second Start (or custom duration) countdown
- Focus Mode for reduced distractions

### Tiny Garden (Optional Easter Egg)
A cozy virtual garden that grows from your real-life Positive Actions.

- Plants progress through stages as you complete activities in Nudge
- No plant death, wilting, or loss of progress from inactivity
- Optional watering, planting, decorating, and rearranging
- Garden Energy earned automatically in the background
- Cute companion (e.g. a small frog) with occasional friendly messages
- Weekly reward summaries based on actual actions, never streaks
- Fully local and completely optional

### Other Capabilities
- Themes, accent colors, and personalization (including thoughtfully designed presets)
- Global search and Quick Capture
- QR sharing/import for routines, checklists, and backpacks
- Template library
- Personal goals and optional Weekly Reset
- Beautiful splash screen + skippable first-time tutorial
- Reminders with quiet hours and full user control
- Data export / import / clear

## Privacy & Local-First Design

- **No account required**
- No mandatory cloud sync, tracking, ads, or analytics
- All personal data (routines, journal, calendar, completions, garden state, etc.) stored locally
- Optional AI features clearly disclose external transmission and require consent
- Journal content is never sent automatically
- Full export and import support

Nudge is intended to be open source.

---

## Design

- Warm, natural color palette with refined glassmorphism and subtle depth
- Apple-inspired elegance: translucent surfaces, soft shadows, tactile controls
- Progressive disclosure — comprehensive features without visual overload
- Fully responsive, mobile-first with polished desktop support
- Light and dark modes designed with equal care
- Smooth, respectful animations (respects reduced-motion preferences)

---

## Technical Notes

- Built as a modern responsive web application with local persistence
- Architecture prepared for future native packaging
- Reliable handling of dates, time zones, and data migrations
- AI integration (when configured) keeps credentials secure and provides graceful fallbacks

---

## Getting Started

1. Open the app
2. Complete or skip the gentle tutorial
3. Optionally set your name and preferences in Settings
4. Create a simple routine or try Bare Minimum / Just One Thing
5. Explore Tiny Garden when you’re ready (it’s always growing quietly in the background)

Welcome back whenever you return. You don’t have to catch up. Just start with today.
